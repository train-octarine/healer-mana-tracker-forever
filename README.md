# Healer Mana Tracker Forever

Shows healer mana in parties and raids in World of Warcraft: Forever. Each bar shows the player's name and mana percentage.

- Track healers by assigned role. A ? marks a possible healer when their role is unavailable.
- Choose to track everyone with mana.
- Hide the panel when there are no healers to track.
- Resize the bars and move the panel.
- Turn bars red below a configurable mana threshold. Choose parties only or both parties and raids. Disabled by default.

## Installation

1. Download the add-on ZIP from [Releases](../../releases) and extract it into `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/`.
2. On the character selection screen, open **AddOns** and enable **Healer Mana Tracker Forever**.

By default, the panel only appears in a party or raid. Use `/hmtracker preview` to see the bars while solo.

## Commands

- `/hmtracker` opens Settings.
- `/hmtracker members` lets you include or exclude individual players.
- `/hmtracker preview` toggles the preview.
- `/hmtracker help` lists all commands.
